Режим генерации: встроенный ImageGen, `photorealistic-natural`.

Для всех пяти запусков использованы три референса:

- `app/assets/exercises/leg_press.png`;

## Сгибание ног сидя

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square Telegram exercise technique card
Primary request: generate a new seated leg curl machine technique card; do not edit the references. Show the same adult woman from the reference images performing a seated leg curl for the hamstrings on a modern selectorized machine.
Input images: Images 1-3 are identity, outfit, gym, lighting, and visual-style references only.
Scene/backdrop: the same clean modern neutral-gray gym with dark rubber flooring and understated purple accents.
Subject: preserve the same woman's recognizable face, body proportions, brunette low ponytail, teal fitted sports crop top, charcoal high-waisted leggings, white training shoes, and small outline heart tattoo near the left collarbone.
Style/medium: polished photorealistic fitness photography matching the references, natural skin and fabric texture.
Composition/framing: 1:1 square card split into two equal vertical panels with a slim clean divider; clear side three-quarter view; full body and the complete relevant seated leg curl machine visible. Left panel is the starting position with legs extended forward but knees softly unlocked. Right panel is the finishing position with both knees flexed approximately 90 degrees and the lower roller pulled down and back.
Technique constraints: back and pelvis fully supported by the seat, thigh restraint positioned above the knees, knee joints aligned with the machine pivot, working roller behind the lower shins just above the heels, hands lightly holding handles, ankles neutral, controlled symmetrical motion.
Lighting/mood: soft neutral studio-gym lighting, calm and encouraging.
Constraints: exact same identity and outfit in both panels; anatomically and mechanically correct seated leg curl; realistic machine linkage and rollers; no text, arrows, logos, labels, watermark, extra people, cropped feet, duplicated limbs, or transparent background.
Avoid: lying leg curl, standing curl, leg extension machine, knees locked in the start, hips lifting, arched lower back, roller on the calves too high, different face, different clothing, loose hair, impossible equipment geometry.
```
